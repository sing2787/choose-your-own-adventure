# The Front Door

You slowly walk toward the front door.

The wooden door is covered in scratches, and the brass handle feels strangely cold beneath your hand.

You turn the handle.

The door opens with a long, loud creak.

Inside, the entrance hall is covered in dust. An enormous grandfather clock stands against the wall, but its hands are frozen at exactly midnight.

Suddenly, you hear footsteps coming from upstairs.

You look toward the staircase and realize that the footsteps are getting closer.

What will you do next?
