# Behind the Mansion

You decide to investigate the strange light.

You walk around the mansion and enter the overgrown garden.

The light is coming from a small stone fountain. At first, it appears to be glowing from underneath the water.

You kneel down and look closer.

At the bottom of the fountain, you see a small silver key.

Before you can reach for it, you hear a branch snap behind you.

Someone—or something—is standing in the darkness.

What will you do next?
