# choose-your-own-adventure
The Abandoned Mansion

It was nearly midnight when you found the mysterious letter lying beneath your apartment door.

There was no name on the envelope. Inside was a single handwritten message:

> "Come to Blackwood Mansion before midnight. What you are looking for is waiting inside."

You had heard stories about Blackwood Mansion. The building had been abandoned for more than twenty years after the Blackwood family mysteriously disappeared.

Despite your better judgment, curiosity got the better of you.

You arrived at the mansion just before midnight. The iron gates were hanging open, and the windows of the old house were completely dark.

As you approached the front door, you noticed something strange. A faint light was coming from somewhere behind the mansion.

You now have to decide what to do next.
